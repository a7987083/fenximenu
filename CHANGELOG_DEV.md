### v1.9.31 device crash evidence

Both supplied ~5 MB games crashed when `Auto Detect / Full Scan` was pressed. The logs isolate a common Full Scan traversal failure rather than a game-specific decrypt failure:

- Both games found their actual semantic menu target while scanning window index 0.
- The runtime-record sample emitted a three-feature `[JAILPATCH-TARGET]` and selector descriptors before the crash.
- The WayOfKings-style sample emitted a four-feature `[JAILPATCH-TARGET]`, `[IGMM-FEATURE-ARRAY]`, and `[IGMM-REGISTER]` before the crash.
- Full Scan nevertheless continued into unrelated windows/targets.
- Neither log reached `[AUTO-TRAVERSAL-END]` or `[AUTO-SCAN]`.
- One crashing sample had `records=0`, so the generic secret-decrypt resolver was never entered. This rules out v1.9.31 decrypt invocation as the common cause of the two Full Scan crashes.

Historical source review found the unsafe path in the v1.9.24 iGMM read-only probe: every custom control target could be recursively deep-probed, and object ivars were enumerated through UIKit/framework superclasses. `@try/@catch` does not protect against `EXC_BAD_ACCESS` generated by invalid raw object graph traversal.
